are opening-only art drafts. The encounter correction below is the adopted
version; the connected tentacle version was rejected.

Deep muted teal foliage, soft mossy green ground, a few pale warm grass highlights and tiny firefly specks. The central and lower foreground are a broad, empty, readable gently lit clearing for two sprites to meet. Keep the middle clear and simple: darker distant trees around the center horizon, quiet horizontal moss ground around the lower third. A small open patch of pale dusk sky at the top center, not a giant moon. No buildings, furniture, roads, water, flowers, giant detailed leaves or props. Gentle, curious, wistful mood, NOT horror. Use roughly 24 colors and large legible pixel shapes. Entire canvas is the background, no margins.
